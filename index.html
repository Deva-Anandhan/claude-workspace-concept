What if your ambition wasn't limited by the team you can afford?

AI can already think, plan and build. Claude Workspace is my concept of what comes next: one place where you ask, approve, and the work gets done, with you in control.

Why I made this

It started with a question I couldn't shake

I'm a GTM Engineer. I build AI employees, systems and automations at scale, and I use Claude almost every day to do it.

Over time I noticed something. If Claude is where I think, plan and build, why does finishing the work still depend on a dozen other apps, each with its own logins, keys and rules?

Read the full essay →

The idea

Ask. Approve. Done.

Agents get what they need to finish a job, and people keep the decisions that matter.

Ask

In chat or mail: fix this contract, renew that tool, set up the new hire.

Approve

An agent does the work and stops at the limits you set: a discount, a purchase, a release.

Done

It finishes, reports back, and every step is on the record.

How it works

You orchestrate. The workspace does.

You stay the one who decides. Everything else, the workspace takes care of.

You
  • Decide what matters
  • Set the budget and the limits
  • Approve the moments that count
  • Own the outcome
asks→←reports back
The workspace
  • Plans the work
  • Uses the tools you already have
  • Gets it done, day and night
  • Keeps a record of every step
